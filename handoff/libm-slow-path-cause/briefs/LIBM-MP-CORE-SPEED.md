## The change

LIBM-SLOW-PATH-CAUSE (handoff/libm-slow-path-cause/report.md §3.6, §3.7, §4 "Cheap wins") found that every special,
long double, `_Float128` and complex call runs the 384-bit `math::mp`. Its time goes into overheads, not
arithmetic:
* erf: 48% `__udivti3` and 18% `pack`.
* expl: 30% `__udivti3` and 28% `pack`.
* atanl: 32% `pack`.

Do these four, each a separate commit with its own counts:

1. **`Mp::div_u64` without u128/u64 software division.** Every divisor is a small integer. Use native `u64`
   division on 32-bit half-limbs when `k < 2^32`, or a precomputed reciprocal and one correction. Keep the exact
   truncation semantics: the documented error of every caller rests on it, so prove the result is bit-identical,
   or re-state the bound.
2. **`pack`**: skip re-normalisation when the top limb's top bit is already set and nothing needs shifting. Check
   that `add`, `mul` and `sqr` call it only when needed.
3. **`mp::ln10()`** re-derives `ln 10` by the full series on every call (log10l = two ln series). Make it a table
   constant generated by `cargo xtask tables` like `MP_LN2`, and audit `mp`, `mpw`, `wide` and `special` for other
   per-call constants.
4. **`elem::finish`** rounds each double-double result through the 256-bit `arith::sum` plus `float::round` (9-20%
   of every double-double path; it is also why sin in upward mode costs 865 instructions against 314 to nearest).
   Add a direct rounding of a normalised double-double into `double`/`float` in each mode, with its proof, and use
   the 256-bit route only for the subnormal and overflow edges.

**Rules:**
* Results must be bit-identical (value and flags) in every mode. The existing accuracy and oracle tests must pass
  unchanged.
* Plants for each change:
  * a 1-off in the reciprocal correction;
  * a `pack` skip that drops a needed shift;
  * a wrong constant limb;
  * a directed-mode sign error in the new rounder.

**JOBS:**
* the gate (direct tier);
* plants;
* the accuracy check (`cargo test -p liella-math` accuracy and oracle suites, including the ignored long double
  differential tests run explicitly);
* counts before and after: `cargo xtask bench --compare --counters --runs 3 cbench_libm` (the rows erf*, lgamma*,
  tgamma*, j*/y*, every `*l`, every `*f128`, and the complex rows).

Expected: about 2-3x on the mp rows, 10-20% on the double-double rows.
