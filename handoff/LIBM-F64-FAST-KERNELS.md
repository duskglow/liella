## Done (this run)
* `fast64::reduce_exp10`: Cody-Waite split of log10(2)/64 in double (the three stored parts of
  `LOG10_2_64_CW`) plus one double-double product by ln10. Proof term by term in its docs: within
  2^-102.7 of exact, normalized, |r.hi| < 0.0054156. It feeds the shared `exp_kernel`, so exp10's
  bound and its constant rounding test are exp's (`exp_eps`, `C_EXP`). Built in both builds
  (inside `kernels!`).
* `fast64::exp10` (all modes, called by the accurate entry) uses it instead of the old
  double-double reduction plus `Dd::mul`. The accurate path is unchanged.
* New in-place entry `fast64::exp10_nearest` plus `fused::exp10_nearest` (FMA), selected in
  `hot::exp10_nearest`; the tsv row `exp10 d` now goes through `elem_fast` (gen.rs regenerated by
  `cargo xtask math-gen`). Domain: 2^-60 <= |x| < 307, integers in [1, 23] refused (10^23 is a
  midpoint). `nearest_stage(6, ..)` counts its stages.
* Results stay correctly rounded, so no differences row changes.
* Tests: `bounds::exp10_reduction_stays_within_its_bound` (vs mp, whole range plus the neighbours
  of k·log10(2)/64); exp10 added to `math_fma_paths` (both builds bit-identical plus the accurate
  path), to the `s10_math_symbols` fast-entry tests and to the ignored 2·10^8 sweep (names/stages).
