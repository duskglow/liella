## What was done
- `layout/contracts.tsv` has 10 rows: FN-ALIGN-EXPORTS (fn-align), CLASSED-XREF (classed-xref), JCC-HOT-ASM (jcc),
  COLD-BSS-LBSS (section-rename), STARTUP-NAMES-WINDOW (object-window), GETTEXT-SLOTS-PAGE (object-align),
  MEMCHR-SSE2-PIN and RAWMEMCHR-SSE2-PIN (section-offset), RAWMEMCHR-LOOP-32 (label-align) and PRODUCT-PATH-REMAP (remap).
- `xtask/src/layout.rs` and `cargo xtask layout-check [--static | <products dir>]`. Each kind calls the existing checks
  (`align::misaligned`, `classed::check_file`, `jcc::check_archive`, `remap::check_dir`). The other kinds read
  `readelf -SWs` (indexed once per file) and `objdump -d -j <section>`. A rule value that differs from the constant
  the existing checker holds is an error, so the manifest and the checker cannot drift apart.
- `cargo xtask so` now calls `layout::check_products` after the last product (either profile). This replaces the direct calls of
  `align::check`, `classed::check_file`, `jcc::check_archive` and `remap::check`. `remap::check` was used nowhere else,
  so it was removed. The `classed-xref` and `jcc-check` subcommands and the static checks are unchanged.
- `static` runs a new `layout` check with these parts:
  - Every row's kind is known and its D-row exists.
  - Every `LAYOUT(<ID>)` tag names a row and sits on a directive line or in the comment lines directly above one.
  - Every site-kind row has a tag. There are 6 tags: scan.S x3, fold.S, startup_names.rs, intl.rs.
  - Rule d is implemented.
- `docs/codemap/README.md` has a new "Layout contracts" section. That file's header says it is generated by a
  scratchpad `codemap.py`, so a regeneration may drop the section (INFORMATIONAL).

symbols are found through their section index, never by section name.
